# Appendix D / Section A Scope Summary

Use this as the scope reference when assessing whether a proposal understands the work. Always rely on the actual RFP and addenda if there is a conflict.

## Project purpose

The City is seeking a consultant to help prepare a five-year Barrie Transit Strategic Plan for 2027 to 2032.

The Strategic Plan will guide:
- Public transportation service delivery.
- Network structure.
- Community connections.
- Fiscal and resource investments.
- Conventional and specialized transit.

## Core work areas

The consultant will help with:
- Peer agency assessment and analysis.
- Stakeholder engagement with staff, riders, community organizations, and regional partners.
- Strategic directions for service, infrastructure, technology, and financial planning.
- A phased implementation roadmap with year-by-year actions and decision points.
- Performance measures and targets.

## Delivery model

The RFP uses a chapter-based delivery model:
- Staff-led: City provides main content; consultant supports formatting and integration.
- Joint: City and consultant both develop content.
- Consultant-led: consultant leads analysis and drafting.

The consultant is expected to:
- Draft and integrate Consultant-led and Joint-developed content.
- Support formatting for Staff-led chapters.
- Submit chapters to the City for review as they are completed.
- Support a cohesive final report.

## Updated timeline

Per Addendum 1:
- Work is expected to take approximately one year.
- Draft Strategic Plan is anticipated in July 2027.
- Council presentation is anticipated in August 2027.

Use this when reviewing F.5 schedule and F.4 understanding.

## Engagement scope

Internal engagement includes:
- MVT and terminal staff workshop.
- City departments workshop.
- Seniors & Accessibility Advisory Committee.
- Analysis and documentation of internal engagement themes.

External stakeholder engagement includes:
- Georgian College.
- Royal Victoria Hospital.
- Metrolinx / GO Transit.
- Simcoe County LINX / Ontario Northland.
- Major employer outreach.

Public consultation includes:
- Virtual Public Open House.
- Online survey and BuildingBarrie.ca content.
- In-person Public Open House as a provisional item.
- Outreach tactics for youth and other groups.
- Analysis of open house and survey results.

Council engagement includes:
- Interim Council workshop.
- Final Council presentation.

Engagement reporting includes:
- Monthly Engagement Digests.
- Thematic analysis.
- Engagement Summary Report.

- Network evolution.
- Transit service standards.

- Fare structure.

- Marketing strategy.
- Customer communications.
- Fleet plan.
- Facilities plan.

## Practical scoring lens

A strong proposal should show:
- Understanding of Barrie Transit, not just generic transit planning.
- Clear approach to the Staff-led / Joint / Consultant-led model.
- Practical schedule for City review and Council timing.
- Specific approach to engagement groups listed in the RFP.
- Capacity for fare analysis, service planning, capital planning, technology, and report production.
- Clear implementation roadmap thinking.
