# Section F Scoring Guide

Use this guide to score the exact worksheet rows for FIN2026-064P.

Scores are 1-10 only. Do not calculate weighted points or totals.

## F.1 Company Overview

Look for:
- Company overview.
- Years in business.
- Number of professional / technical employees.
- Primary professional services.
- Transit planning capability.
- Office locations.
- Project team resources by location.
- Subconsultants, legal relationship, expertise, services, and percentage involvement.

Minimum:
- Proponent has at least 5 years providing related services.
- Proponent has the capability, resources, and public sector transit or transportation planning experience needed for the deliverables.

Preference:
- More than 5 years providing related services.
- Other relevant expertise or depth of experience that would benefit the City.

Improvement examples:
- Missing staff counts.
- Office/resource split is unclear.
- Subconsultant role or percentage involvement is unclear.
- Transit planning experience is stated but not supported.

## F.2a Project Team Overview

Look for:
- Organization chart.
- Named project team members.
- Assigned roles.
- Team reporting structure.
- How team skills map to the deliverables.
- Previous experience working together.

Minimum:
- Clear team structure for the deliverables.
- Clear assignment of Project Manager and Senior Transit Planner.

Preference:
- Proposed team has worked together on similar transit or transportation planning work.
- Strong transit-specific depth across the team.

Improvement examples:
- Org chart missing or unclear.
- Roles overlap or are vague.
- Team experience together is not shown.
- Key roles are named but availability is unclear.

## F.2b Project Manager

Look for:
- Project Manager summary and CV.
- Education, credentials, and relevant experience.
- Two similar project examples completed within the past 5 years.
- Project name, client, location, dates, value, role, and responsibilities for each example.
- Confirmation of availability for the full project.

Minimum:
- At least 5 years of relevant experience in a project management or similar leading role.
- Experience managing multi-disciplinary teams.
- Experience with stakeholder engagement.
- Experience presenting to elected officials or senior decision-makers.
- Experience with quality control, schedule, and budget delivery.
- Availability for the full project.

Preference:
- More than 5 years of relevant experience.
- Leading role on a public sector project.
- Experience getting Council approval for a transit strategic plan or similar transportation strategy.
- Steering committee or advisory group workshop experience.
- Experience with municipal transit agencies using a contracted operations model.
- PMP, RPP, P.Eng., or other professional qualifications that benefit the City.

Improvement examples:
- Project examples missing dates, values, or role details.
- Council or senior decision-maker experience is not shown.
- Contracted operations model experience is not shown.
- Availability is not confirmed.

## F.2c Senior Transit Planner

Look for:
- Senior Transit Planner summary and CV.
- Education, credentials, and relevant experience.
- Two similar project examples completed within the past 5 years.
- Project name, client, location, dates, value, role, and responsibilities for each example.

Minimum:
- At least 5 years of relevant transit or transportation planning experience.
- Experience with transit service planning, route design, frequency analysis, or network restructuring.
- Experience with peer benchmarking or best practices reviews across multiple comparable transit agencies.

Preference:
- More than 5 years of similar experience.
- Direct experience with public sector transit strategic plans.
- Ontario municipal transit experience.
- Fare analysis, fare capping, or fare technology assessment experience.
- RPP, P.Eng., or other professional qualifications that benefit the City.

Improvement examples:
- Project examples are not clearly similar.
- Peer benchmarking experience is not shown.
- Fare analysis or fare technology experience is not shown.
- Ontario municipal transit experience is limited or unclear.

## F.2d Additional Team Members

Look for:
- CVs for additional team members and subconsultants.
- Roles and responsibilities.
- Qualifications, technical skills, and relevant experience.
- Similar project experience.

Minimum:
- Team covers the full scope, including financial analysis, fare modelling, capital planning, and report production.
- Each team member has relevant qualifications, technical skills, and experience.
- Roles are clearly defined.

Preference:
- Experience with transit fare and revenue analysis.
- Transit technology systems experience, such as fare systems, real-time information, or scheduling software.
- Graphic design or report production strength.
- Public sector experience, especially municipal transit or transportation work.

Improvement examples:
- Financial analysis, fare modelling, capital planning, or report production coverage is thin.
- Subconsultant roles are unclear.
- CVs do not connect experience to this project.
- Too much work appears assigned to unnamed or junior resources.

## F.4 Project Understanding and Approach

Look for:
- Understanding of objectives and scope.
- Understanding of Staff-led, Joint, and Consultant-led chapter model.
- Methodology for major work streams:
  - Peer benchmarking and policy research.
  - Stakeholder and public engagement.
  - Service planning and network development.
  - Fare strategy and financial analysis.
  - Capital and technology planning.
  - Plan integration and report production.
- Collaboration model with City staff.
- Approach for Staff-led inputs and Joint work.
- Project-specific risks and mitigation.
- QA/QC approach.

Minimum:
- Clear understanding of the project scope and chapter delivery model.
- Defined approach to achieving the deliverables.
- Clear method for stakeholder engagement and public consultation.

Preference:
- Practical understanding of shared chapter delivery.
- Realistic project risks and mitigation.
- Value-added approaches at no extra cost.
- Engagement methods that reach non-traditional transit users.

Improvement examples:
- Approach is generic and not tied to Barrie.
- Staff-led / Joint / Consultant-led model is not addressed.
- Engagement approach misses key groups.
- Risks are generic or not tied to the project.
- QA/QC process is vague.

## F.5 Work Plan and Schedule

Look for:
- Work plan.
- Gantt chart or similar schedule.
- Tasks, milestones, critical path, meeting dates, key events, timelines, dependencies, and deliverable submissions.
- City review periods for drafts or chapters.
- Uncosted Time Task Matrix.
- Team member hours by task for Strategic Plan Development and Engagement Plan.
- No hourly rates, costs, or pricing in the uncosted matrix.

Minimum:
- Work plan addresses all project chapters.
- Key milestones, meetings, sequence, and City review periods are shown.
- Schedule shows delivery within the intended 12-month timeline.
- Effort allocation by team member and task is reasonable.
- No more than 15% of total estimated hours assigned to unnamed resources.

Preference:
- Logical sequencing with clear dependencies.
- Realistic timing for City-supplied information and City review.
- Clear and legible Gantt chart and Uncosted Time Task Matrix.
- Work plan supports quality and consistency.

Improvement examples:
- Schedule does not align with July/August 2027 Council timing.
- City review periods are missing or too short.
- Critical path or dependencies are unclear.
- Uncosted Time Task Matrix is missing or hard to read.
- Pricing appears in the uncosted matrix.
- More than 15% of hours are assigned to unnamed resources.
