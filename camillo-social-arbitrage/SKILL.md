# Camillo Lens — Simple Connections and Speculative Information Edge

Read ../investment-strategy-lanes/SKILL.md. This is an integrated research method, not a second mandatory process or a copy-trading instruction. Use the shared strategy_case identity through every handoff.

## Start with five questions

1. **What changed?** Describe the observation and its baseline. Separate event, disclosure, observation and discovery dates. A known fact can become newly important.
2. **What are the dots?** Use three to five plain steps connecting the observation, changed behaviour/capability, the company and a potentially material consequence. Prefer the simple obvious beneficiary when the evidence supports it; obscurity is not an edge.
3. **What may investors be missing?** Contrast the accessible expectations evidence with the proposed interpretation. Label sampled commentary versus measured consensus; an unmoved stock or absent article does not prove ignorance.
4. **What is the bet?** Separate known facts, attributed claims, supported inference and uncertain magnitude/adoption/duration. State the strongest alternative explanation and a falsifier.
5. **Why now?** Name the recognition event/process, review window, remaining opportunity and what could make it too late or wrong.

A good thesis fits in three sentences. Use the three most decision-relevant checks before expanding. Do not turn simple reasoning into shallow fact-checking, but do not bury a live hypothesis in low-value research. Be willing to conclude **SPECULATIVE CANDIDATE** while future outcomes remain uncertain.

## Ground truth and connections

Use accessible firsthand product demonstrations, user experience, independent reviews, adoption/retention clues, public partner relationships, operating evidence and original disclosures. Disclose actual sample and source coverage. Launch attention is not automatically purchases, repeated use or profit. Review negative cases and the strongest confounder. A podcast disclosure is self-reported, not a verified fill; discussion is not purchase, and one cohost's trade is not another's.

Illustrative reasoning patterns, not live trade claims:
- Product quality beyond expectations -> engagement and word of mouth -> greater lifetime economics -> reassessment.
- Mainstream uptake of a new service -> more workloads -> greater use of a verified supplier relationship -> a possible earnings/expectations surprise.
- Lower cost per task -> newly viable uses -> uncertain change in total demand -> possible supplier benefit. Demand expansion must exceed price/cost offsets; it is not guaranteed.

Do not invent a contract, exclusivity, supplier share or technical relationship. Do not require future multi-year retention data for a prelaunch hypothesis. Rough scale and retained economics should be plausible; exact future profit can remain an explicit uncertain bet.

## Outputs and boundaries

Normally return a compact connection brief:

**Strategy / Case / Stage / Horizon**
**The idea:** three sentences maximum.
**The dots:** A -> B -> C.
**What may be missed:** with expectations-source limitations.
**Known / inferred / the bet:** separate the three.
**Three decisive checks:** completed findings or named open checks.
**Why now / next review / falsifier.**
**Information-edge judgment / instrument judgment / portfolio permission.**
**One next action.**

Positive information-edge judgment does not require a low multiple, DCF discount, legacy Core buy price or reported earnings confirmation. It does require more than an exciting story. Price/remaining payoff, recognition timing and loss risk still matter to the actual investment.

Radar detects/routes; RWC verifies starting evidence and tests the inference; Full Underwriting in CAMILLO mode evaluates the bet and security; challenge and allocation keep their independent responsibilities. See ../full-underwriting/references/camillo-speculative-underwriting.md. Missing allocation data is not a reason to suppress the research candidate, but prevents invented size or execution permission.

Learn from dated predictions, failed connections and timing/instrument errors as well as wins. Public information and independent reasoning only. No automatic leverage, trade, live holding relabel or conversion of a failed speculation into Core.
