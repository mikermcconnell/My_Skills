# Full Underwriting — strategy-aware decision router

Read ../investment-strategy-lanes/SKILL.md before selecting the underwriting mode. Begin every report with Strategy / Case / Stage / Horizon. Do not classify solely by ticker, instrument or holding period.

## Universal near-term expectations lens

Every Full Underwriting should include a concise, dated **next-quarter expectations** check when the issuer has an upcoming reported quarter and credible consensus data are accessible. Research what analysts currently expect for the next reported quarter and compare that baseline with the underwriting thesis.

At minimum, capture the most decision-relevant available expectations: revenue, EPS/earnings, operating income or margin, management guidance, and the segment/KPI expectations that actually drive the case. Include the expected reporting window/date when known. Use reputable current sources and distinguish company guidance, published consensus, individual analyst estimates and our own derived analysis. Do not invent a consensus figure when coverage is unavailable.

Then ask:
- What does the market appear to expect **next quarter**, not just over the long term?
- Does our thesis imply a plausible beat/miss or, more importantly, a different KPI/mix/guide than consensus?
- Which result would accelerate recognition, delay it, or falsify part of the case?
- Is the next quarter likely to reveal the thesis, or is the evidence horizon later?

This is an expectations benchmark, **not an earnings prediction requirement**. Do not manufacture a beat/miss call when the edge is unrelated to the next quarter. A one-quarter surprise does not automatically validate or invalidate a multi-quarter thesis. Preserve the distinction between near-term expectations, long-term intrinsic value and Camillo recognition payoff.

## CORE — Long-term portfolio

Read and apply BASELINE_WORKFLOW.md in full and its relevant references. This preserves the existing core business, reverse-valuation, capital-structure, scenario, return-hurdle, time-to-resolution, clinical overlay, challenge, sizing-handoff and monitoring workflow.

## CAMILLO — Speculative information edge

Read references/camillo-speculative-underwriting.md and ../camillo-social-arbitrage/SKILL.md. Use that mode's requirements and compact output instead of forcing the Core valuation/readiness/posture definitions onto the speculative case. The Camillo decision engine is **change significance -> economic bridge -> market recognition / expectations gap -> next-quarter expectations -> recognition path -> remaining recognition payoff -> failure / instrument risk**. Retain universal source, security, current-price, material financing/dilution, instrument, failure, challenge and allocation controls. Use relevant baseline/reference sections as support, not a requirement to complete a full Core valuation before a speculative judgment.

No low multiple, DCF discount, analyst-target discount, legacy Core buy threshold or reported financial confirmation is mandatory for a positive Camillo edge judgment. Price still matters as **remaining recognition payoff** and downside, not as a Core cheapness veto. Never claim something is definitively 'priced in'; infer a dated recognition state from evidence and identify the specific consequence that may still be under-recognized. Separate change significance, information edge, security/instrument suitability and portfolio permission. No invented portfolio percentage or risk budget.

A same-issuer Core HOLD and Camillo EXIT REVIEW can coexist for different verified cases/expressions. Do not overwrite the Core baseline, double-count lots or infer a live strategy conversion. Unknown strategy mapping retains existing accepted rules pending classification.

Event-Trade Underwriting may support discrete short-duration execution/payoff questions without changing strategy identity. Event Reaction mechanical lots retain their separate manifest. Challenger and Allocation must receive the same strategy contract. No execution or final account-specific sizing approval occurs here.
