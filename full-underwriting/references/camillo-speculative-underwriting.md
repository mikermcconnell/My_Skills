# Camillo speculative underwriting mode

Revision: September 24, 2026. Read ../../investment-strategy-lanes/SKILL.md. This is the Camillo mode of Full Underwriting, not the Event Reaction mechanical strategy and not a mandatory conventional valuation rebuild.

## Decision question

**Is the proposed change in market understanding sufficiently plausible and consequential to justify considering this exact security/instrument from today's price, before full confirmation?**

Retain the RWC starting evidence and case identity. Recheck current decision-sensitive facts. Distinguish inference from fact. Keep the opening answer simple and preserve an actual speculative judgment.

## Required assessment

1. **The information edge.** State what changed, three to five dot-connections, evidence about current expectations, the uncertain bet, and why the potential recognition is still ahead. Comprehensive consensus data is not mandatory; disclose a provisional expectations-gap hypothesis rather than pretending it is measured.
2. **Materiality and competing effects.** Show a defensible rough scale/capture check and the strongest reason the company might not benefit. Do not force point estimates or a five-year model when they add no decision value. Financing, dilution, partner claims and business offsets matter when they could defeat the bet.
3. **Failure, delay/no recognition, and successful recognition.** Explain each outcome, its evidence, indicative security payoff when supportable, and its timing. Add a breakout case only if it represents a distinct outcome. State probability ranges or break-even odds when defensible, not invented precise odds. A price target must identify its basis; unknown payoff inputs remain unknown.
4. **Remaining payoff at the current price.** Distinguish attractive information from an attractive instrument. Test whether the anticipated outcome appears already reflected and whether failure/delay risk overwhelms remaining upside. A Core DCF discount, fixed Core entry threshold, mandatory low multiple or fixed Core annualized hurdle is NOT a gate. Respect any actually approved Camillo-specific rule. Do not annualize a short speculative payoff as if it were repeatedly available.
5. **Instrument and clock.** Record exact listing/currency, current quote and timestamp, intended horizon, recognition process/window, dated review and loss/falsification policy. For options assess strike, expiry, premium at risk, liquidity, volatility, breakeven and timing. An option expiring before a product launch can be intentional when the underwritten recognition event is earlier; do not reject it for the launch date alone. Do reject/review a genuine mismatch to its intended recognition window. A correct long-term company thesis cannot rescue an unsuitable contract.
6. **Challenge and allocation.** Independently challenge the weakest connection, expectations gap, failure path and timing. Mark UNCHALLENGED when that pass is unavailable. Send failure/delay losses, event-gap risk, horizon, exact exposure and unresolved inputs to Portfolio Capital Allocation. Do not invent the user's speculative loss budget or recommended weight.

For a genuinely short-duration discrete event, obtain Event-Trade Underwriting's execution/payoff expertise as needed while preserving CAMILLO strategy identity. Do not automatically reroute every Camillo case based on a calendar threshold alone.

## Three separate verdicts

**Information edge:** SPECULATIVE CANDIDATE / WATCH / NO DISTINCTIVE EDGE / INVALIDATED, with confidence and reason.

**Security/instrument:** actionable for speculative allocation review / unsuitable expression / insufficient remaining payoff / unresolved, with the actual price and timing basis.

**Portfolio permission:** pending allocation / within verified approved limits / blocked or unavailable. Only a completed allocation review can support the latter determinations. A positive research judgment is not a BUY instruction.

These labels are user-facing explanations, not new backend enums. When persistence requires an existing posture, use the compatible allowed posture with explicit CAMILLO-mode rationale only if its requirements are honestly satisfied. Do not coerce a Camillo result into a Core valuation slot, overwrite a Core baseline, or fabricate mandatory DCF/target fields. Preserve it as a separate dated case when storage cannot represent the distinction.

## Output and monitor handoff

Begin: Strategy / Case / Stage / Horizon; then the three verdicts and one next action. Include the simple connection, known/inferred/bet split, expectations-gap evidence, scenario payoff/uncertainty, strongest challenge, failure and delay, recognition/review dates, exact instrument and allocation constraints. No compulsory Core-style valuation chapter.

Handoff: case revision; edge state; next recognition/evidence checkpoint; falsifier; evidence-expiry/review deadline; intended hold-through or reduce-before policy if relevant; instrument expiry; required evidence for adding; remaining payoff; accepted price/risk conditions if any; and aggregate issuer/cluster exposure where verified.

Recognized information, a fading edge, missed timing or inadequate remaining payoff triggers review of the Camillo case even while the company's long-term thesis remains intact. Do not silently extend the horizon, average down, roll an option or convert to Core. Preserve the original bet and record any deliberate renewed decision.
