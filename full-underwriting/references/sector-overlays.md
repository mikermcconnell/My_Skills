# Sector Overlays

Select only the overlay(s) that materially fit the security. These are minimum questions, not rigid templates.

## Mining / resource developers

### Geology

Assess:

- resource size and grade
- measured / indicated / inferred mix
- reserve status
- drilling density and continuity
- depth and geometry
- metallurgy and recoveries
- resource conversion
- exploration potential

Do not assume every resource ounce becomes mineable or economically recoverable.

### Mine economics

Assess:

- production profile
- mine life
- recovery
- operating cost / AISC where appropriate
- initial capex
- sustaining capex
- royalties and taxes
- NPV / NAV
- IRR
- payback
- commodity-price sensitivity

### Development credibility

Assess:

- PEA / PFS / FS stage
- permitting
- infrastructure
- metallurgy
- geotechnical risk
- processing solution
- construction / underground-development status
- schedule realism
- operator / management execution history

### Financing

Model cash, remaining capex, debt capacity, equity requirement, warrant dilution, royalties/streams, and post-financing share count.

### Valuation

Use project NAV/NPV as the main intrinsic framework where appropriate and cross-check with P/NAV, EV/resource unit, peers, and relevant transactions.

Never compare market cap directly with project NPV without accounting for financing, dilution, execution risk, time to production, permitting, resource confidence, royalties, and taxes.

### Strategic investor / takeover

Separate:

- technical validation
- governance / information rights
- strategic optionality
- actual takeover evidence

Do not place acquisition premium in the Base case merely because a strategic investor owns shares.

## Biotech / clinical-stage healthcare

Assess program by program:

- mechanism and biological rationale
- trial stage
- endpoints and clinical relevance
- efficacy versus standard of care
- safety / tolerability
- durability
- enrollment and trial design
- regulatory path
- manufacturing / CMC
- competitive pipeline
- addressable patients
- pricing and penetration
- exclusivity / IP
- milestone / royalty obligations
- cash runway and financing

Use probability-adjusted NPV / pipeline SOTP and cross-check with market-implied probability, comparable licensing, or precedent transactions.

Separate:

- clinical evidence confidence
- regulatory probability
- commercial probability

For clinical holds or safety events, distinguish class precedent, route/vector/product-specific causality, actual patient outcomes, regulatory requirements, and the information needed to resolve uncertainty.

### Pre-pivotal speculative underwriting

When RWC advances a clinical-stage security **before** the pivotal readout, do not treat missing Phase 3 results as a reason to abandon underwriting. Treat pivotal uncertainty as the central scenario variable.

Build at least these economically distinct states when material:

1. **Pivotal failure / clinically non-viable** — include residual cash/platform/program value, financing obligations and realistic post-failure dilution or restructuring.
2. **Ambiguous / technically positive but commercially weak** — marginal effect size, safety/tolerability, label, durability, dosing, manufacturing or differentiation disappoints despite avoiding outright failure.
3. **Base clinical success** — clinically/regulatorily credible outcome with realistic commercial penetration and financing.
4. **Strong differentiated success** — better efficacy, safety, convenience, durability, label or commercial profile than the Base case.
5. **Exceptional / platform read-through** only when evidence supports a distinct upside path; do not manufacture a fifth case for symmetry.

Explicitly reconcile:

- Phase 2 effect-size shrinkage / winner's curse;
- pivotal design and population differences;
- larger-N and longer-duration safety risk;
- regulator alignment versus sponsor interpretation;
- competitor/class successes and failures;
- cash runway through readout and filing;
- expected dilution before/after readout;
- manufacturing/CMC readiness;
- what intermediate catalysts de-risk clinical probability versus only improve commercial quality;
- current enterprise value and market-implied success/quality assumptions.

Use rNPV / pipeline SOTP or another appropriate method, but show the **break-even pivotal-success probability** and the **probability required to clear the return hurdle** whenever the pivotal outcome dominates value.

### Speculative starter eligibility

Full Underwriting may mark a pre-pivotal case:

`Pre-pivotal speculative starter: ELIGIBLE | NOT ELIGIBLE | UNRESOLVED`

`ELIGIBLE` requires all of the following:

- RWC survives with a credible clinical/regulatory mechanism and explicit failure case;
- current price leaves positive expected value or a defensible return-hurdle case under probability ranges;
- financing to the key readout is understood well enough to model dilution;
- pivotal design/translatability and safety risk are explicit;
- the Bear/failure path is tolerable only at a deliberately small loss budget;
- a named next evidence ladder exists for adding, holding, trimming or exiting;
- the conclusion is independently challenged when available before normal capital-allocation advancement.

`ELIGIBLE` does **not** imply a normal target weight. The position is a speculative starter until evidence earns more size. Portfolio Capital Allocation owns the exact weight and may still choose zero.

Do not use a small size to excuse a weak thesis. If the expected value is poor, financing is uncontrolled, or failure residual value is unknowable, use WATCH/PASS rather than hiding uncertainty inside position sizing.

## SaaS / software

Assess:

- ARR / recurring revenue quality
- net revenue retention
- gross retention / churn
- customer growth
- ARPU / expansion
- gross margin
- sales efficiency
- CAC payback
- RPO / backlog quality
- FCF margin
- SBC dilution
- pricing power
- product displacement risk
- AI-driven cost or product changes where material

Avoid treating high gross margin or TAM as proof of durable growth.

Valuation should connect growth, durable margin, reinvestment, dilution, and FCF rather than relying on revenue multiples alone.

## Semiconductors / AI infrastructure

Assess:

- end demand and workload growth
- utilization
- capacity constraints
- ASP / pricing
- product mix
- gross margin
- node / architecture transition
- competitive performance
- customer concentration
- supply chain and packaging
- capex intensity
- inventory
- useful life / depreciation
- infrastructure payback / ROIC where relevant

For AI infrastructure, explicitly test:

`Demand -> utilization -> revenue -> gross profit -> operating profit -> cash return on invested capacity`

Falling unit compute/token cost is not automatically bearish if usage and utilization expand faster.

Separate announced capex, deployed capacity, utilized capacity, contracted demand, and realized returns.

## Hyperscale cloud

Assess:

- cloud revenue growth
- AI versus non-AI growth where disclosed
- backlog / RPO
- capacity constraints
- capex and depreciation
- utilization
- hardware useful life
- gross / operating margin
- customer commitments / prepayments
- custom silicon economics
- cash conversion
- incremental ROIC / payback

Do not equate customer spending commitments with immediate revenue unless the source supports it.

## Consumer / retail / e-commerce

Assess:

- traffic
- conversion
- order frequency
- active customers
- GMV / units
- average order value
- take rate
- gross margin
- fulfillment / shipping cost
- CAC and advertising intensity
- repeat behavior
- inventory / working capital
- competitive pricing
- regulation / tariffs / de minimis exposure

For marketplace share claims, seek direct evidence of traffic, orders, users, units, GMV, or share. Do not infer one company's share gain solely from a competitor's slowdown.

## Marketplaces / fintech / brokers

Assess:

- funded customers / active users
- assets / balances
- deposits / net new assets
- transaction volumes
- take rate / monetization
- net interest economics
- retention / cohort behavior
- cross-sell
- regulatory capital
- credit losses where relevant
- customer acquisition cost
- SBC / dilution
- cyclicality of revenue
- dependence on market activity / crypto / rates

Separate structural platform growth from temporarily favourable trading volumes or interest rates.

## Banks / insurers

Assess:

- capital adequacy
- funding and deposit mix
- net interest margin
- loan / premium growth
- credit losses / reserves
- delinquency trends
- duration / asset-liability mismatch
- underwriting quality
- ROE / ROTCE
- book / tangible book growth
- reinsurance where relevant
- regulatory constraints
- capital return

Use sector-appropriate valuation such as P/TBV, P/B, normalized P/E, residual income, or dividend-based methods.

## Industrials

Assess:

- volume / price
- backlog quality
- book-to-bill
- capacity
- utilization
- margins
- raw material / labour sensitivity
- working capital
- capex
- aftermarket / recurring mix
- customer concentration
- cyclicality
- order cancellation risk

Normalize cycle-sensitive earnings before applying valuation multiples.

## Commodity producers

Assess:

- realized commodity price
- production volumes
- reserve / resource life
- cost curve position
- sustaining and growth capex
- balance sheet
- hedging
- royalties / taxes
- geopolitical exposure
- project pipeline
- capital allocation

Run commodity-price sensitivities. Do not capitalize peak-cycle earnings as if permanent.

## REIT / real estate

Assess:

- occupancy
- rent growth
- lease expiries
- tenant quality
- NOI
- AFFO
- cap rates
- same-property growth
- debt maturity ladder
- fixed / floating mix
- interest coverage
- development commitments
- asset values

Use NAV, P/AFFO, and cap-rate sensitivity as appropriate.

## Event-driven / regulatory special situations

When a dated regulatory, transaction, legal, or binary event dominates value, incorporate:

- verified event terms
- key dates
- success / delay / break outcomes
- downside convention
- timing cost
- market-implied probability
- analyst probability only when supportable
- break-even probability
- what evidence changes the probability

Use a focused event-analysis workflow when process/legal mechanics dominate the underwriting, then integrate the result into Full Underwriting.
