# Consolidated opportunity assessment guidance

Read this when comparing possible projects, investments, or career opportunities.

- Define the desired outcome and constraints before brainstorming.
- Generate options across adjacent domains rather than variations of one idea.
- Score options on fit, evidence, upside, cost, reversibility, and time to learn.
- Identify the cheapest experiment that could disprove the leading option.
- Finish with a ranked shortlist and a concrete next action.
