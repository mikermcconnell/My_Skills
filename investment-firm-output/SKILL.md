# Investment Firm output contract — version 14

## Explain the subject before the signal

For every unfamiliar product, company, technology or category, begin with **What it is** in one or two plain-language sentences before rankings, growth observations or investment implications. Explain what people do with it and define the category, rather than replacing one unfamiliar term with another. Identify the verified maker/operator when useful; distinguish that from an unresolved publicly traded beneficiary. A short familiar analogy may help, but cannot imply unverified features or shared ownership.

Briefly explain the verified business model when relevant: who pays, what they buy, and whether advertising is involved. Unknown ownership or monetization stays explicit and does not block an early signal. Cite the description to accessible original sources. Do not invent context to satisfy a template.

Card order: **What it is -> What we observed -> Why it may matter / the connection -> The uncertain bet -> Next check**. Translate unexplained codes such as P2 RWC into a readable next action; keep actual priority/stage codes in supported metadata or parentheses. Prefer 'No publicly traded beneficiary identified yet' over 'public mapping unresolved'.

Normally use 60–110 words for familiar subjects; allow roughly 100–170 words for a newcomer needing context. Do not sacrifice understanding to a word budget. Later updates retain a brief name-plus-description reminder instead of a repeated primer. Apply this to Core, Camillo and the opening context of requested research/underwriting.

## One integrated report per slot

Title: **Investment Firm — Radar — YYYY-MM-DD — HH:MM Toronto**. State the intended slot, actual information cutoff, session basis and material coverage limitations. Scheduled start is not a guaranteed completion/delivery minute. Use verified premarket or labelled previous close at 08:00; actual regular-session data at 11:00/15:00 where open, otherwise dated last-session data. Never invent weekend prices or call 15:00 a closing-market report.

Retain three main sections:
1. **New news and opportunities.** New Core and Camillo observations, including unfamiliar products, source limitations and tentative investment connections.
2. **Changes to existing investment cases.** Material thesis/decision changes, actual research progress, resolved/rejected connections, consequential blockers and next evidence/checks. Put synthesis here, not in a second report.
3. **Stock monitor — Buy / Hold / Wait / Sell.** Core and Camillo monitoring and supported decisions; exclude routine Event Reaction lots from the table. A new confirmed mechanical trigger may appear as a short alert callout, not a full sleeve inventory.

Preserve explicit **CORE — Long-term portfolio** and **CAMILLO — Speculative information edge** labels within each, with truthful compact no-change/not-assessed coverage. Shared evidence appears once, with different strategy implications cross-referenced. A short action summary may lead when a verified decision or trigger needs attention; do not duplicate the same story and table into multiple newsletters.

Every slot includes useful decisions, genuine research progress and the next check when material. At **15:00**, add a brief day-to-date synthesis of what actually changed in the decision set and what comes next; do not replay all morning headlines. Friday 15:00 also absorbs the existing AI Efficiency Watch weekly breadth and calibration, with its fixed-cohort/backfill/cost/quality safeguards unchanged. Late nonurgent findings enter the next regular slot, not a new 15:20 report. An urgent condition retains the existing exceptional-alert path.

## Core and Camillo content

Surface research-worthy Camillo EARLY / BUILDING / RWC NOW observations in the run that finds them, using behaviour-led or capability-led framing. One traceable source, unknown ticker, missing retention/earnings/quote/active monitor or an above-Core-target price is not an automatic veto. Facts, company claims, sponsorship and forward speculation remain distinct; unsupported metadata-only material stays a source-verification lead. Keep at most three decisive follow-up checks unless a genuine issue warrants more. No required signal count or numerical stock score.

A materially new implication of an old relationship may be NEW CONNECTION — EXISTING EVIDENCE. Preserve original evidence dates, compare the prior interpretation and reject semantic duplicates. Keep source novelty separate from connection novelty. No fake new announcement or guessed supplier relationship.

Apply ../news-radar-investing/references/camillo-source-monitoring.md, its source pack and google-trends-connection.md. Distinguish real chart/index changes from baseline-only, repeated data, provider revisions and incomplete coverage. Ranks and search indices are not sales or customer counts. Preserve stable IDs, source/capture dates, geography, device, category, sample scope and normalization through research and underwriting. Include one compact retrieved/unchanged/partial/blocked/pending coverage note, not raw app lists. Registration or a submitted job is not successful source access.

Core keeps its accepted business/valuation/return gates. Camillo keeps its information-edge, instrument/payoff and portfolio-permission judgments. Both retain challenge, exact-security and risk controls. Full requested research remains full research; consolidation does not automatically execute queued RWC/underwriting or approve capital.
