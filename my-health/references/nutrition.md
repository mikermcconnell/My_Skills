# Nutrition approach

Default to sustainable food quality rather than precision tracking.

- Notice whether meals included a meaningful protein source, fibre-rich carbohydrate, vegetables or fruit, and adequate fluids.
- Consider meal timing, appetite, digestive tolerance, activity, sleep, and the stored health profile.
- Avoid moral labels such as "good" or "bad" food. Focus on patterns and the next realistic choice.
- Do not infer calories, macros, portions, deficiencies, or intolerance from a vague description.
- Use genetics only when an approved finding has a sufficiently direct and well-supported implication.
- Recommend one-variable experiments when useful, define the period and signal to observe, and do not claim causation from a short personal trend.
