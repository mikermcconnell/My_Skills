## Scheduled output

The prior per-scan Radar title, lead-routing table, always-visible eleven-lane list, full unchanged price table and repeated mini-reports are retired from scheduled chat delivery. They remain available as audit/research detail where relevant.

A producer publishes only a qualifying **Investment Firm — Action Alert**. Otherwise persist findings for the designated weekday **Investment Firm — Daily Brief**, and do not notify. Material findings must not disappear silently if both canonical and journal persistence fail: use the shared contract's concise reporting-gap fail-safe. Do not send 'no update' or successful-run acknowledgements.

The designated Daily Brief uses: **Your decisions; What changed; Research progress; What comes next**. The standing Decision List is one current reporting view with explicit blocked/unverified states, not a separate recurring report or financial baseline. Full research remains available on explicit request.

No output change authorizes a new fair value, price threshold, kill criterion, review date, thesis probability, holding, trade, application deployment, or automatic retirement of an unverified risk channel.
