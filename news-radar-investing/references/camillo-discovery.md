Version 1; approved September 24, 2026. Strategy: CAMILLO_SPECULATIVE. Read the shared investment-strategy-lanes contract. This contract owns Camillo discovery, early surfacing, source-access diagnostics and connection novelty. It does not change Core valuation, Event Reaction mechanics, allocation limits, schedules or execution authority.

> Show what might be starting, connect the facts simply, and name the next check. Do not wait for a finished investment story.

## 1. Protected search, not merely a label

Within EVERY existing 08:00, 11:00 and 15:00 Toronto run, after bounded P0 triage and before routine case expansion/quote assembly/persistence repair, complete both protected discovery obligations: the existing Core broad-source pass and this complementary observation-first pass. Reuse evidence fetched for both; do not repeat the entire news scan. Neither strategy may consume the other's discovery budget. A genuine P0 emergency may interrupt, with exact skipped coverage and recovery recorded.

Read camillo-source-register.json. The default bounded batch is four differentiated queries/feed checks: (1) unseeded consumer behaviour/repeated use; (2) unseeded capability or newly easier/cheaper activity; (3) switching, abandonment or a negative counter-signal; (4) rotating non-AI demand/supply or practitioner evidence. These are coverage obligations, not four required findings. At least one actual non-finance consumer/practitioner source and one original product/release/demo source must be examined when accessible. Never substitute four stock-news queries or four copies of one story. If no suitable original source is accessible, mark that obligation PARTIAL rather than fill it with publicity.

Use a rolling source window from the last verified successful source/family cutoff with overlap for indexing delay. A recency filter is not a publication timestamp. First-run recovery may examine seven days, explicitly labelled; it does not certify a week of complete coverage. Undated or older material can remain a lead with DATE UNKNOWN / LATE DETECTION / HISTORICAL SUPPORT as appropriate. Do not discard a new connection because an underlying partner announcement is older than a fixed age cutoff.

Always revisit due fast signals and persistent source gaps. Slot emphasis is consumer/capability discovery at 08:00, independent checks and switching/practitioner evidence at 11:00, and latest deltas/recognition risk at 15:00; all three still perform the four protected checks. Rotate non-AI categories using the register. A missed category carries forward before rotation advances; repeat failed sources only within bounded retry limits. No additional tasks or continuous-access claim.

## 2. Two valid entry paths

**BEHAVIOUR_LED:** people describe or demonstrate changed use, purchasing, payment, repetition, switching, cancellation or abandonment.

**CAPABILITY_LED:** a traceable demonstration, distribution change or release makes something newly possible, easier or cheaper. Future adoption is explicitly the bet; lack of retention data is not a reason to erase a prelaunch lead.

Classify observed evidence separately as ATTENTION / INTENT / PURCHASE / REPEATED_USE / PAYMENT / SWITCHING / ABANDONMENT / CAPABILITY / DISTRIBUTION / SUPPLY_CONDITION. These are descriptive metadata, not new backend enums. They are not an obligatory ladder: capability can matter before purchase; negative signals matter too.

Search meanings such as 'use every day', 'bought another', 'replaced my subscription', 'started using at work', 'no longer need', 'stopped using', 'back in stock', 'wait time increased' and 'now possible without a specialist'. Vary wording and query structure. Exact phrase matching, a ticker dictionary and the word 'stock' must not be inclusion requirements. Search outside current holdings, AI themes, Chris Camillo and investing communities; map securities after observing the change. Product names supplied by the user create a targeted branch, not fulfillment of independent discovery.

## 3. Early surfacing gate

Surface **CAMILLO — EARLY** when there is:
- a traceable public observation or an explicitly attributed original claim;
- a specific potentially meaningful change (or a clearly labelled baseline-unknown change hypothesis);
- a plausible consequence worth investigating;
- a concrete next check capable of changing the interpretation.

One credible origin CAN qualify. Unknown ticker, unknown market awareness, no financial conversion, no long-term retention, no price quote, no active monitor and a price above Core's buy target are NOT automatic suppression reasons. Preserve unresolved mapping with a named ownership/supplier test. Do not invent a public beneficiary.

A source claiming a capability is evidence of that claim, not proof the capability works. Label COMPANY CLAIM / FIRSTHAND ACCOUNT / DEMONSTRATION OBSERVED / SECONDHAND UNVERIFIED. Sponsored origin need not erase a verifiable feature launch, but it cannot establish organic adoption. Bare 'this stock will moon', popularity without a consequence, a repeated price explanation or an unsupported supply relationship does not qualify as a confirmed observation. Worthwhile unverified claims go to SOURCE VERIFICATION, not a falsely confirmed EARLY card. Retain raw rejected/deferred leads with reasons in supported manifest state.

Use existing routes. P2 is normal for an early consequential signal with a named check; P3 for a weaker but testable observation. P1 can be warranted by ONE strong discontinuity when materiality and a short recognition window justify prompt RWC; several independent sources or elapsed weeks are not prerequisites. Record the reason. P0 remains credible urgent risk. Early surfacing does not start a worker or approve a trade.

## 4. Mandatory dot-connecting, including old facts

For each worthwhile observation, write a three-to-five-link chain. Ask: who benefits directly; who must spend or supply more; which verified existing relationship becomes more important; what may be displaced; and what investors might notice next. Do not force five securities or a complicated chain. An obvious liquid company can be the right hypothesis. Distinguish money flowing through a supplier from profit retained by it; only a plausible rough consequence is needed at discovery.

Perform a bounded old-fact lookup for relevant ownership, distribution, partners, suppliers, installed base or previously disclosed capacity. Preserve each relationship's source/date, scope and limits. A relationship does not prove exclusivity, product-specific sourcing, supplier share or retained margin. Explicitly label unresolved links.

Maintain TWO novelty tests:
1. source/observation novelty, deduplicated by original origin and atomic claim;
2. connection novelty: **old evidence + new observation or newly reasoned implication + materially different consequence**.

An already-seen URL must not suppress a genuinely new connection. Conversely, paraphrasing the same mechanism is not new. Retrieve prior case/sequence/fallback before deciding, reuse its canonical case identity when available, and state precisely what interpretation changed. Mark NEW CONNECTION — EXISTING EVIDENCE, not a new company announcement; preserve original first-seen dates. The helper's hash only detects exact normalized duplicates; semantic dedup remains required.

No numeric score. Prioritize potential consequence, strength of the observation, recognition urgency and value of the next check, not obscurity, follower count, mention volume, ticker recognition or Core cheapness.

## 5. Trajectory and expectations memory

Reuse existing Signal Sequences and the Reporting Journal/authorized manifest. Do not create another investment database. Retain observation IDs, independence groups, source/publication/collection/first-seen dates, original baseline, comparison method, geography/category/cohort and revised hypothesis. If no baseline exists, say BASELINE NOT ESTABLISHED; never infer acceleration from one snapshot.

Compare like with like: product/SKU, geography, chart/category, query/topic, time window and data method. Different query samples or differently normalized series cannot establish growth rates. Reposts across platforms remain one origin. New accounts do not prove independent customers. Do not infer demographic characteristics; broader adoption needs reported use cases or defensible cohort evidence. Search-interest, rank and sentiment are not sales or retained usage. Stockouts can be supply problems; discounts can be seasonal; compare the strongest alternative explanation.

Preserve two separate narratives: what people/products appear to be doing, and what accessible investor commentary/guidance appears to understand. At discovery the expectations gap may be only a hypothesis, with scope disclosed. No price reaction is not proof of ignorance, and a price rise is not proof the opportunity is finished.

Set next check from signal speed: next existing slot for fast recognition, 1–2 days for fast usage/availability checks, days-to-week for practitioner/channel evidence, or a named release/report window. These are planning defaults, not promises. Missing evidence is a review event, not automatic deterioration; never silently push a due date forward. Keep a reviewer decision for DORMANT/INVALIDATED and the original reason. Old relationships can remain reference evidence even when an associated trading hypothesis expires.

## 6. Source access and reuse

The source register is an executable search plan, not proof every source is connected. Each attempt must retain source ID, actual query/URL, method, retrieved_at, publication window inspected, status, pagination boundary, collection limitations and last successful cutoff. CHECKED means the defined sample was actually read, not exhaustive platform coverage. Listing/title access does not imply body/transcript access. Cached/undated results cannot establish an intraday delta.

Reuse readable existing Investor social-arbitrage evidence and signals as supplemental inputs. The September 24 code audit found personality/stock-seeded Firecrawl defaults, ticker/trend-dictionary filters, fixed-date exclusion and scores biased toward trade language. Those outputs MUST NOT be the only input or eligibility gate for this pass. Read raw public evidence through authorized web/connectors and preserve research-only records in existing supported state/fallback. Do not bypass platform blocks or authorize API costs to repair missing coverage.

Known-channel YouTube discovery should use its verified uploads feed/playlist when accessible, not search alone. Never imply captions or full audio were reviewed from the episode description. Dumb Money is an attributed idea/learning source: extract observation/connection/disclosed action, distinguish cohosts and self-reported trades, and ask whether our independent source plan could have found the upstream evidence. It does not satisfy independent discovery. User observations are optional, not required fuel.

If backend fields require ticker or disallow connection metadata, keep the unmapped observation in the authorized dated manifest/fallback, linked to the parent case. Do not use a dummy ticker, unsupported enums, overwrite Core, retag lots, activate monitors or migrate records. A store outage cannot silence a supported signal in the report; disclose saving limits. No private journal IDs, tokens, holdings or live-case contents enter public GitHub.

## 7. Visible card and handoff

Inside the existing CAMILLO subsection under New news and opportunities, surface all research-worthy incremental leads, usually 60–110 words each; no minimum or hard maximum that hides material information:

**CAMILLO — EARLY / BUILDING / RWC NOW**; BEHAVIOUR_LED or CAPABILITY_LED; case/sequence ID.
**Observation / source / date:** what was actually seen, with source status and sample limitation.
**Connection:** A -> B -> C; possible company or mapping unresolved.
**The bet / what may be missed:** inference, not claimed certainty.
**Next check / due window / falsifier:** one concrete decision-changing test.
**Stage:** research lead, not approved trade; actual route and work state.

One compact coverage note distinguishes checked/no change from incomplete/unavailable; never manufacture an EARLY item to fill the lane. Shared Core news is cross-referenced rather than repeated. Non-price research cases remain visible under the existing Camillo monitoring rules, separate from active canonical price/risk monitors. Preserve exact strategy/case/instrument identity.

RWC receives the original observation, its source status, chain, original baseline, uncertainty, independence groups, unresolved company mapping, expectations-gap hypothesis and at most three decisive checks. RWC can advance a supported speculative inference to Full Underwriting — CAMILLO MODE. Keep the existing three verdicts: information edge; instrument/payoff; portfolio permission. Nothing here lowers truth standards or changes Core underwriting, Event Reaction, risk budgets or execution rules.

## 8. Verification and learning

Use scripts/camillo_discovery.py for a reproducible query plan and record/coverage checks when execution is available; otherwise apply the same contract and label automated checks NOT RUN. The helper does not perform web retrieval, assess source truth, verify organic demand or establish an investment edge. Its outputs are diagnostic, not production enum definitions or trade decisions.

Run evals/test_camillo_discovery.py for deterministic routing/date/dedup/coverage regressions. Keep synthetic fixtures separate from actual financial history. During the first five completed post-change Radar runs, inspect actual query evidence, original sources read, first-observation surfacing, unknown-ticker retention, new connections on old evidence, non-AI coverage, due checks, source failures and unchanged Core/Event Reaction handling. Add the compact result to existing brief/weekly calibration, not another task.

Measure detection delay separately from surfacing delay, independent versus user/podcast-seeded discovery, resolved/failed/unresolved research, false-lead reasons, collection gaps and missed-case causes. A changed prompt or passing unit test is not proof of live coverage or performance. Historical replay must use a predefined mixture of successful, failed, promotional and ordinary cases with point-in-time sources and hidden later outcomes; do not claim a return backtest or earlier access from retrospective web search. No fixed lead yield target.
