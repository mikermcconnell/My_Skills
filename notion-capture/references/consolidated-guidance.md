# Consolidated Notion workspace guidance

Read this when reviewing or cleaning existing Notion content rather than only capturing new notes.

- Search and inspect the current schema before proposing changes.
- Separate overdue or incomplete work from records that are merely old.
- Present a dry-run list before bulk edits, moves, or deletions.
- Preserve stable identifiers and relationships between projects and tasks.
- Make destructive changes only after explicit confirmation and verify the resulting records.
