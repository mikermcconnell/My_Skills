# Portfolio Capital Allocation — strategy-aware

Read ../investment-strategy-lanes/SKILL.md and BASELINE_WORKFLOW.md plus relevant references. Core retains every inherited precondition and sizing rule. Camillo's accepted underwriting input is ../full-underwriting/references/camillo-speculative-underwriting.md, not a mandatory Core DCF, Core buy threshold or Core annualized-return test.

For Camillo require a supported current-price security/instrument assessment, credible failure/delay/recognition payoff, material financing and gap risks, recognition/review window, challenge status, current portfolio/cash when needed, and an explicit approved speculative loss budget before precise sizing. Do not invent a default sleeve percentage, per-trade budget, leverage allowance or risk tolerance from 'higher risk'. Missing budget permits labelled sensitivity and a pending allocation decision, not suppression of a promising information-edge conclusion or a fabricated allocation.

Use loss-budget sizing and the existing concentration, liquidity, funding, evidence-earned staging and execution controls. Aggregate all actual Core, Camillo, Event Reaction and other exposure by issuer/factor; do not count one lot twice or assume separate legal holdings for two analytical cases. A different strategy label is not diversification. Reconcile conflicting actions on the same lot before a proposal.

Additional Camillo capital requires named evidence and remaining-payoff/risk review. A recognized/fading edge may justify reduction review even when Core remains intact. Strategy conversion needs a fresh destination-strategy underwriting/allocation decision and preserved history; it cannot rescue a failed trade automatically.

Keep the inherited FUND / FUND SMALL OR STAGED / WAIT / HOLD CASH OR FUND ALTERNATIVE / TRIM OR REALLOCATE / REJECT outcomes and no-execution/account/tax boundaries. Send strategy and exact case/lot scope through the handoff and monitoring record.
