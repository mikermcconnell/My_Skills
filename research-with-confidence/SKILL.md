# Research With Confidence — strategy-aware

Read ../investment-strategy-lanes/SKILL.md first, then BASELINE_WORKFLOW.md and its task-relevant references. The baseline remains the complete inherited RWC workflow. For CAMILLO also read ../camillo-social-arbitrage/SKILL.md and ../news-radar-investing/references/camillo-discovery.md.

Begin every investment report with Strategy / Case / Stage / Horizon. Shared facts can support two cases but Core and Camillo conclusions and handoffs remain separate. In a single-lane report label the other not assessed rather than silently rejecting it or duplicating work.

Core retains its existing mechanism, materiality, expectations, expression and valuation-readiness tests. Camillo uses the same truth standards but can advance a coherent, sourced forward inference before adoption, retention or earnings are confirmed. Separate facts, attributed claims, inference and the bet. Concentrate on the three decisive checks and proportionate alternatives rather than turning every early lead into a full dossier.

## Observation-first handoffs

An EARLY Radar handoff is a research question, not completed validation. Preserve original observation and first-seen/source dates, source-access and sponsorship limitations, behaviour-led/capability-led path, prior baseline or absence, old-fact/new-consequence chain, independence groups, expectations-gap hypothesis and named next tests. Correct them explicitly rather than silently rewriting what was originally predicted.

A missing ticker can make company/owner/supplier mapping the first research question; do not reject the raw insight or insert a guessed/dummy symbol. One original demonstration can warrant research without several later adoption datasets. Verify a claimed relationship independently before treating it as fact; future share of demand remains an assumption where undisclosed. A sampled investor narrative is not market-wide consensus.

Return a source-verification result when the starting evidence cannot be established. Do not equate unverified source claims with uncertainty about a well-sourced future inference. Reject an incoherent/refuted connection, not uncertainty itself. Keep failed and unresolved cases for calibration; no retrospective baseline rewriting.

Use ADVANCE -> FULL UNDERWRITING — CAMILLO MODE visibly when warranted, with compatible native routing fields and strategy annotation. Missing conventional cheapness, a Core target crossing or reported financial proof is not an automatic Camillo stop. Remaining payoff and instrument approval belong downstream. Preserve separate confidence in facts, connection, expectations gap and recognition timing.

Retain factual independence, counterfactuals, owned-instrument defense and all no-trade/no-final-sizing boundaries from the baseline. No new API enums, issuer-wide Core baseline overwrite, holdings retagging or unsupported claim of a running worker.
