# Consolidated observability and incident guidance

Read this when defining production signals or responding to an operational incident.

- Define user-centered service indicators and a small set of actionable objectives.
- Instrument logs, metrics, and traces around critical paths and failure boundaries.
- Alert on symptoms requiring action, with owner, severity, and runbook.
- During incidents, stabilize service first, keep a timestamped decision log, and communicate impact plainly.
- Follow with a blameless review that assigns concrete prevention and detection actions.
