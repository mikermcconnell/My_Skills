# Underwriting Challenger — strategy-aware

Read ../investment-strategy-lanes/SKILL.md and BASELINE_WORKFLOW.md. Core retains the inherited audit unchanged. For Camillo also read ../full-underwriting/references/camillo-speculative-underwriting.md and challenge that mode rather than applying Core cheapness/readiness rules by default.

For Camillo independently test the observed facts, weakest dot-connection, strongest competing explanation, expectations-gap evidence, materiality/capture, recognition window, remaining payoff, failure/delay losses and exact instrument. Audit any supplied valuation or probability arithmetic, but do not demand a Core DCF, low multiple or crossed Core entry price as a prerequisite. Uncertain future adoption is the bet, not an automatic factual failure. An invented supplier relationship or no credible path to benefit is different.

Preserve source independence, cutoff, security/claims audit where material, genuine failure analysis, disagreement reconciliation and the baseline SURVIVES / SURVIVES WITH REVISIONS / NOT DECISION-READY / FAILS outcomes. State Strategy / Case / Stage / Horizon. A positive information-edge verdict may coexist with a failed instrument or pending allocation. No position sizing, automatic conversion, original-record rewriting or execution.
