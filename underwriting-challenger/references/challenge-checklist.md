# Independent Challenge Checklist

## Price and security

- [ ] Correct ticker, listing, currency, share class, and current price.
- [ ] Basic, diluted, and post-financing share counts reconciled.
- [ ] Enterprise value and all material claims reconciled.
- [ ] Cash-flow ownership by asset, territory, indication, or subsidiary confirmed.

## Source and claim quality

- [ ] Primary source inspected for every load-bearing fact.
- [ ] Shared-origin articles deduplicated.
- [ ] Company claims distinguished from independent evidence.
- [ ] Calculations reproduced with units and denominators.
- [ ] Information cutoff preserved; ex-post evidence labelled.

## Priced-in view

- [ ] Independent reverse valuation or market-implied probability performed.
- [ ] Original target or verdict was not used as the starting anchor.
- [ ] Disagreement is attributed to a specific input or method.

## Model audit

- [ ] Revenue, margin, FCF, probability, or asset bridges are internally consistent.
- [ ] Financing, dilution, and timing interact correctly.
- [ ] The same success is not counted in multiple line items.
- [ ] Cross-check is genuinely independent rather than the same assumptions in another formula.
- [ ] Terminal value and multiple expansion do not dominate without justification.

## Breakpoints

- [ ] One or two highest-value assumptions identified.
- [ ] Conclusion-changing breakpoint calculated.
- [ ] Entry-price sensitivity calculated when price-sensitive.
- [ ] Delay and annualized-return effects tested.

## Failure path

- [ ] Strongest credible disconfirming fact identified.
- [ ] Bear case includes a realistic causal failure sequence.
- [ ] Liquidity, financing, or forced dilution under stress is considered.
- [ ] Residual value is not assumed without evidence.

## Hurdle and alternative

- [ ] Actual hurdle used or unavailable status disclosed.
- [ ] Closest realistic alternative compared.
- [ ] Confidence, liquidity, duration, downside, and correlation included.

## Reconciliation table

Use:

| Area | Original | Challenger | Evidence / calculation that resolves it | Final treatment |
|---|---|---|---|---|

Only unresolved material disagreements should block a Decision-ready conclusion.
